The housing market around Geneva and the Pays de Gex is extremely competitive, which unfortunately attracts scammers targeting vulnerable newcomers.

## Warning Sign: Upfront Payments

Agencies and landlords (such as _BC Immobilier_ in Ferney-Voltaire or _Matera_) may demand safety deposits or the first month's rent **prior** to signing the contract or viewing the flat.

> [!WARNING]
> Never transfer deposit money or rent before signing a lease agreement and inspecting the property. A landlord who wants money first "because they cannot draft the contract yet" or "until you get insurance" is a red flag — walk away.

## Safe Practices

- **Sign First**: Insist on signing the lease agreement before making any bank transfer. For example, users dealing with _BC Immobilier_ successfully insisted they would not transfer funds until the contract was signed. The agency agreed, signed first, the transfer was made, and keys were handed over during inspection.
- **Normal FR sequence**: sign lease → pay deposit (and often first month) → get keys. Deposit before move-in is normal; deposit **without** a signed lease is not.
- **Insurance timing**: Home insurance is usually arranged **after** signing (you often need the contract to take out the policy). Provide the landlord the insurance attestation before move-in — not as a reason to pay before signing.
- **Physical Offices**: If the agency has a physical local office and you have visited the flat in person, it is generally safe, but you should still follow the "sign before paying" rule.
- Prefer seeing the empty flat after the previous tenant has left before signing and paying, when possible.

## Visale (France)

[Visale](https://www.visale.fr/) is a free state-backed deposit guarantee for eligible tenants in France. Some landlords require Visale approval before finalising the lease — that can be legitimate paperwork, but it is **not** a reason to send rent or deposit without a signed contract.

## Geneva régies and RC privée

Swiss agencies often ask about *RC privée* (personal liability insurance). French mandatory housing insurance is not the same thing. Attaching a liability-insurance quote (e.g. from AXA) with the application is often enough to proceed.
