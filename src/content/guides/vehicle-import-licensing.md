Bringing a vehicle to the CERN area involves navigating customs, registration plates, and insurance rules across two countries.

## Green Plates (Transit / Customs CD equivalent in France)

Registering a car on custom green plates (VAT/customs duty exempt) is highly bureaucratic.

- **Exporting from Switzerland to France**: Buying a used car in Switzerland and exporting it to France on green plates takes **2 to 3 months** via the CERN Car Plates Services.
- **Warning**: During this transition, Swiss export plates might expire. You will be unable to drive unless French temporary CPI (WW/Pink) plates arrive.
- **Insurers**: Major insurers like _AXA_ and _Allianz_ accept green plates.

### Carte internationale d'assurance (green-plate proof)

CERN often asks for the international insurance card (_carte internationale d'assurance_ / green card), not just your regular contract PDF.

- On **AXA**, it is often available in the online account under *Certificat d'assurance*.
- On **Allianz**, the online account may only show the contract. Ask in the shop (e.g. Ferney or Saint-Genis) — staff can usually print the card on the spot. Pointing them at their own website wording that they provide it also helps if support is confused.

## English-speaking car insurance (France)

Recommended options from locals:

- **Fab French Insurance** — English-speaking department.
- **AXA Saint-Genis** — agents who speak English; quotes often start online, then an agent calls back.
- **Allianz Ferney / Saint-Genis** — English speakers available; prefer going **in person**. Email-only claims/setup can take weeks (multi-day gaps between replies).

## Proof of address for French registration

To put a French-plated car in your name (or complete related admin), you typically need a recent *justificatif de domicile*:

- Phone bill (often the easiest; electronic bills with your address are usually accepted).
- Bank statement, electricity bill, or rent receipt from an agency.
- If bills are not in your name: [*Attestation d'hébergement*](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/simulateur/calcul/AttestationHebergement) filled by the person who pays them, plus their ID and a bill in their name.
- Some people successfully use a **Revolut** address verification flow when paper bills are awkward.

Official overview of accepted proofs: [service-public.fr – Justificatif de domicile](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F14807).

## UK Cars & Insurance

Expats bringing UK-plated cars to France face insurance limitations.

- Standard French insurers only cover UK plates for 90 days.
- **FabFrenchInsurance** and the English-speaking branch of **Allianz** can provide full C3 cover for over a year on British plates.
- **No Claims Bonus (NCB)**: Online quotes are very expensive (~900€). Going **in person** with paper proof of your UK No Claims Bonus can drop the price to ~500€-600€.

## Driving Licenses

Under a CERN legitimation card, you are **not required** to exchange your foreign driving license for a local one. It remains valid for the entire duration of your stay.

- **Driving Schools**: Highly rated schools include [My-DS](https://my-ds.ch/en) (Geneva, English-friendly theory test, also offers motorcycle courses) and [L2D](https://l2d.ch).
- **Test Languages**: In Geneva, the theory test is available in English, German, Italian, or French. However, the **practical exam is strictly in French**.
