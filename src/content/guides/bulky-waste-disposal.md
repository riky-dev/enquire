When moving in or out of the Pays de Gex, disposing of broken or unwanted furniture requires following local rules.

## Disposal Methods

- **Déchetterie (Recycling Centre)**: Broken items must be taken to a local _déchetterie_. This requires a car and a local waste access card (badge).
- **Emmaüs**: For reusable items in good condition, Emmaüs will pick them up from your home for free.
- **Curbside Pickup**: Pays de Gex offers a free "Allô Encombrants" bulky waste collection service (primarily for those without a suitable car or with reduced mobility).
  1. Fill out the online form on the [Pays de Gex Waste Portal](https://mesdechets.paysdegex.fr) or call **04 50 20 65 86**.
  2. Wait to be contacted by the service to schedule a collection date.
  3. Place up to 3 bulky items on the street the evening before the scheduled collection.
