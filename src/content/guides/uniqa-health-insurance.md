The CERN health insurance (Uniqa) provides excellent coverage, but there are several subtle rules you must follow to ensure your claims are approved.

## Dermatologist Visits

Uniqa covers dermatologist visits, but **only if medically necessary** (not aesthetic).

- **Rule**: Ask your dermatologist to write a brief note stating that the treatment/visit was medically required, and attach this note to your Uniqa claim.

## Prescription Glasses & Sunglasses

Glasses and lenses are covered when they provide **visual correction**. Uniqa confirmed that the correction may be calculated by an **ophthalmologist or an optician**, and must be clearly mentioned on the invoice — a doctor's prescription is **not** required.

- Prescription sunglasses (dark lenses with correction) are reimbursed the same way as clear lenses.
- Practical tip: ask the shop to note on the invoice that the product is a corrective medical optical device in your name.
- Ordinary suncream is generally **not** reimbursed as "prevention" (prevention cover focuses on things like cancer screenings and some vaccinations).

## Dental Checkups

Routine dental checkups are covered under Uniqa. If you hit the Swiss-side dental ceiling, French-side dentists are often cheaper for remaining treatment — see the Dentists category on the services page.

## Booking appointments

- **Switzerland**: [OneDoc](https://www.onedoc.ch) is commonly used to book specialists.
- **France**: [Doctolib](https://www.doctolib.fr) is the usual equivalent.

## Extreme Sports (Skiing, Mountain Biking)

Uniqa covers medical expenses from accidents, but **does not cover search & rescue or repatriation**.

- **Recommendation**: Join the **Austrian Alpine Club** (ÖAV) or French equivalent. Membership is cheap and includes comprehensive mountain rescue and repatriation insurance.

## Swiss Insurance Cancellation

Once your Uniqa coverage starts, you can cancel your Swiss health insurance (LAMal). Your Swiss insurer (not Uniqa) will retroactively cancel your policy and refund any overlapping premiums once they receive the official exemption certificate.
