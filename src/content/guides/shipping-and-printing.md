Practical options for sending bulky bags abroad and printing a few pages without CERN printers.

## Shipping large luggage (Mondial Relay)

[Mondial Relay](https://www.mondialrelay.fr) works well for big luggage from Saint-Genis / Geneva toward other European countries (e.g. Poland reported ~**4–5 days**).

- **Locker → locker**: often allows printless deposit via QR/code.
- If the locker rejects a large bag, go to a **Mondial Relay point**, show the QR code, and staff will take the package and print the label.
- There is a point in **Saint-Genis** (and others nearby such as Thoiry).

## Printing outside CERN (France)

- **La Poste Saint-Genis** (119 Avenue de la République) — self-service machine; bring a USB stick and pay at the machine. Check opening hours (closed Sundays / some holidays).
- **Maison de la Presse** (near Charly's, Route de Lyon) — roughly **0.35€** per B&W page; you can connect your phone to the printer Wi‑Fi. See the Printing category on the services page.
