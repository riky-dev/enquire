Navigating cross-border finances is one of the first major tasks for a CERN newcomer. Depending on where you choose to live, your banking options and strategies will differ.

## The Yuh Bank & French Residency Issue

**Yuh Bank** is a very popular Swiss digital bank option with zero maintenance fees. However, **Yuh is no longer available to individuals residing on the French side of the border (Pays de Gex)**. If you reside in France, Yuh will reject your application or close your account. Referral sharing from a French-resident account may also be restricted.

## Banking Alternatives for French-Side Residents

- **Dukascopy**: An official Swiss digital bank. They are fully digital, accept French addresses, and work well for newcomers.
- **UBS**: A traditional Swiss bank. They accept French residents but have poor reputation for fees and bureaucracy. There is a branch on the Meyrin site useful for short-term associates who need a Swiss account quickly.
- **[Ibani](https://www.ibani.com/en/referral?code=UmUqMFaY)**: A well-known specialized service that provides you with a Swiss (CH) IBAN. It automatically converts your incoming CHF salary to EUR and forwards it to your home EU bank account. Popular with French-side residents who mainly spend in EUR.
  - _Caveat_: This is only cost-effective if you spend most of your money in EUR. If you spend in CHF, you will lose money on double conversion (~2% fee range reported).
  - _Note_: The link above is a referral link — signing up through it may give you (and me) a small bonus.

## Wise & Revolut

- **Wise**: Popular for converting CHF → EUR at low rates. App/virtual cards work well; physical cards are also available. Note that a Wise CHF balance often has a **GB IBAN**, so it is generally **not** suitable for receiving CERN payroll (which requires a CH IBAN).
- **Revolut**: Useful for spending across currencies. If Swiss or French card verification fails in-app, contact support and push for manual verification — people report it resolving within ~30 minutes when you insist. Premium/Metal plans help if you exchange often.

## Currency Exchange Strategy

To minimize exchange fees when converting CHF salary to EUR, many newcomers use **Wise** in combination with their bank. Transferring CHF to Wise and exchanging to EUR at Wise's low rates (typically 0.4% - 0.5% fee) is significantly cheaper than standard bank rates. For cash euros, withdrawing from an ATM is often better than a bureau de change — but fees depend on your bank.

## Payroll reminder

CERN pays remuneration and subsistence allowances by transfer to a **Swiss bank account (CH IBAN) only**, not in cash. See the [admin e-guide on payments](https://admin-eguide.web.cern.ch/en/procedure/payment-remuneration-and-contractual-subsistence-allowance).
