Small practical notes that come up constantly in the CERN newcomer chat.

## Board games

- **Contrebande de Jeux** in Saint-Genis — dedicated board-game shop (see Shopping services).
- **Fnac Balexert** (Geneva) — also stocks games like Catan if you are already on the Swiss side.

## Electronics: France vs Switzerland

Electronics are often cheaper in Switzerland because of lower VAT (~8% vs ~20% in France), especially for phones, laptops, and similar high-ticket items.

- Check **[Galaxus](https://www.galaxus.ch) / Digitec** (same group) and compare with French Amazon / Darty / Fnac.
- In-store **MediaMarkt** / **Interdiscount** can be convenient for same-day needs, but prices are not always better than Galaxus.
- TVs are a mixed bag — sometimes French retail wins; always compare the exact model.

## SwissPass / SBB ticket delays

If you buy a ticket at a machine and load it onto a SwissPass, it can take a long time to appear in the SBB app. Keep the purchase receipt; inspectors usually understand when the digital ticket is still syncing.

## Tap water in Saint-Genis

Tap water in Saint-Genis is drinkable ([eaufrance quality data](https://www.services.eaufrance.fr/commune/01354/2024#qualite-eau)). It can taste of chlorine and is somewhat hard (limescale). Leaving a jug in the fridge for ~1 hour or using a filter helps.
