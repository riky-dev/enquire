BarberWoods is a well-liked Geneva barbershop. Locals recommend booking via their app, which shows which barber speaks which language — useful if you want English.

### Booking Tips

Install their booking app from [barberwoods.net](https://www.barberwoods.net/) or call ahead. Ask for a barber who matches your language preference.
