Péclôt 13 is a self-managed cooperative that repairs bikes and sells restored used bikes (including many recovered via the Geneva bike pound / police). The Grottes workshop is near Cornavin (tram 18 / Lyon area).

### Why go

- Good option if you need a **cheap used bike** and marketplace listings are empty.
- They also repair most bike types, including e-bikes (check the Jonction atelier for e-bike diagnostics).

### Notes

English is not guaranteed — bring basic French or a translation app. Check [peclot13.ch](https://peclot13.ch/) for opening hours per location; they vary by atelier.
