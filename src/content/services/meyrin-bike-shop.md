Meyrin Wheels & Gears is a highly recommended bicycle shop located just a short ride from CERN's Meyrin campus. They specialize in commuter bikes, e-bikes, and quick repairs.

### CERN Newcomer Hack
Show your **CERN access card** (or legitimation card) at the counter to get an automatic **10% discount** on all parts and accessories, and 5% off service labor!

### Booking Tips
They get extremely busy during the spring and summer onboarding waves. It's highly recommended to book your service slot online via their website instead of showing up unannounced.
