Newsagent / stationery shop near Charly's in Saint-Genis. Handy for **short document printing** without going to CERN.

### Pricing & tips

- About **0.35€** per black-and-white page (as reported by locals).
- You can connect your phone to the printer Wi‑Fi directly.
- Closed on some Sundays/holidays — check before you rely on it for an urgent admin deadline.
