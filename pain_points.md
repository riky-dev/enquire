## 1. Banking & Financial Logistics

### The Yuh Bank & French Residency Issue

- **Context**: Yuh Bank (a popular Swiss digital bank option) is no longer available to individuals residing on the French side of the border (Pays de Gex). UBS is a common alternative but has poor reputation/fees.
- **Recommendations/Workarounds**:
  - **Ibani**: Gives you a Swiss (CH) IBAN and automatically converts the incoming CHF salary to EUR and sends it to a home bank account (e.g., an Italian or other EU bank).
    - _Caveat_: Only good if you don't spend most of your money in CHF; otherwise, you lose on double conversion.
  - **Dukascopy**: An official Swiss bank. Works well and is fully digital/available.
  - **Wise**: Used in combination with other accounts. E.g., transferring CHF to Wise and then exchanging to EUR at Wise's low rates (0.4% - 0.5% fee).

### Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) Letters

- **Context**: Banks (e.g., UBS, Yuh) send letters to newcomers requesting their Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN). This causes panic for new arrivals.
- **Explanation & Solutions**:
  - **For Swiss Residents**: Your TIN is your Swiss social security (**AVS**) number. This can be obtained by contacting the **Cards Service** at CERN.
  - **For French Residents**: You do not receive a French TIN until you file your first tax return. UBS/banks are generally fine if you tell them you haven't filed your first return yet.
  - **Information Links**:
    - [CERN KB Article on TIN](https://cern.service-now.com/service-portal?id=kb_article&n=KB0010295)

### Taxes & Declarations

- **Context**: Newcomers struggle with French tax forms (which are often blank paper forms when sent for the first time).
- **Details**:
  - Declaration is usually due around May.
  - Dividends must be declared. In France, there is no minimum tax-exempt threshold for dividends. However, there is an option to opt into income-dependent taxes on dividends (which saves money for low-income brackets).
  - **Links**:
    - [Income Tax Declaration in France (CERN Admin e-guide)](https://admin-eguide.web.cern.ch/en/procedure/income-tax-declaration-france)
    - [Income Tax Declaration in Switzerland (CERN Admin e-guide)](https://admin-eguide.web.cern.ch/en/procedure/income-tax-declaration-switzerland)

## 2. Housing Search & Admin Hacks

### Security Deposits and Scams

- **Context**: Agencies (e.g., _BC Immobilier_ in Ferney-Voltaire, _Matera_) frequently demand safety deposits or first month's rent payments _prior_ to contract signing or viewing.
- **Real Chat Experiences**:
  - One user insisted to _BC Immobilier_ that they would not transfer money until the contract was signed. The agency agreed to sign first, after which the transfer was made, and keys were handed over during inspection.
  - _Rule of thumb_: If the agency has a physical office and you have visited the flat, it is generally safe, but users should still insist on signing first if possible.

### Swiss Housing Application Requirements

- **Context**: Swiss landlords/agencies (_Régies_) require intense vetting.
- **Details**:
  - They demand a **non-pursuit extract** (_Office des Poursuites_) showing you have no unpaid debts in Switzerland.
  - Some ask for private CERN HR contact details (name, email, phone) to confirm contract and salary details. Newcomers can use the official HR career page contact points [here](https://hr.web.cern.ch/my-career-and-experience-cern) or contact their department's dedicated HR representative.

### Home Insurance (Assurance Habitation) in France

- **Context**: Short-term interns need insurance that matches their short lease terms.
- **Recommendations**:
  - **Lemonade**: Popular among interns. Costs around 12€/month + a mandatory 6€ government tax (total ~18€/month). Easy to cancel for short-term stays (e.g., 5 months).
  - **Luko (by Allianz)**: Similar pricing, but some users noted they may require a one-year minimum contract.

## 3. Driving, Cars, and Custom Plates

### Green Plates (Transit / Customs CD equivalent in France)

- **Context**: Registering a car on custom green plates (exempt from VAT/customs duty) is highly bureaucratic.
- **Details**:
  - Buying a used car in Switzerland and exporting it to France on green plates takes **2 to 3 months** via the CERN Mobility Center.
  - During this transition, Swiss export plates might expire, leaving the driver unable to drive unless French CPI (temporary/WW) plates arrive.
  - WW/Pink plates: Issued by dealers but may require VAT payment upfront unless processed via CERN.
  - **Insurers for Green Plates**: _AXA_ and _Allianz_ accept green plates.

### UK Cars and No Claims Bonuses (NCB)

- **Context**: UK expats bringing UK-plated cars to France face insurance hurdles.
- **Details**:
  - Standard French insurers only cover UK plates for 90 days.
  - **FabFrenchInsurance** and **Allianz (English-speaking branch)** can provide full C3 cover for over a year on British plates.
  - **NCB Verification**: Online quotes are very expensive (~900€). Going **in person** with paper proof of a UK No Claims Bonus can drop the price to ~500€-600€.
  - _Indian insurance caveat_: In India, insurance is tied to the car owner (often a parent) rather than the driver, making it hard to prove claims-free history online. In-person negotiation is required.

### Driving Licenses & School

- **Context**: Legality of foreign licenses and taking tests in Switzerland/France.
- **Details**:
  - Under a CERN legitimation card, you are **not required** to exchange your foreign license for a local one. It remains valid for the duration of your stay.
  - Exchanging is possible if you register with the local commune.
  - **Driving School Recommendations**:
    - [My-DS](https://my-ds.ch/en) (Geneva): Instructors speak good English and explain the process.
    - [L2D](https://l2d.ch)
  - **Test Languages**: In Geneva, the theory test can be taken in English, German, Italian, or French. However, the **practical exam is strictly in French**.
  - **Links**:
    - [Official CERN guide on Foreign Driving Licenses in France](https://home.cern/news/official-news/cern/foreign-driving-licences-france)
    - [Geneva License Exchange Info](https://www.ge.ch/echanger-son-permis-conduire-etranger)

## 4. Legitimation Card Disasters

### Stolen vs. Lost Legitimation Cards

- **Context**: The difference between losing a card and having it stolen while traveling abroad is massive due to Swiss bureaucracy.
- **Detailed Incident**:
  - A user had their Swiss card stolen in Spain right before their flight back. Because they had no time to get a Spanish police report before boarding, they returned to Switzerland without one.
  - The Cards Service refused to issue a replacement without a police report from the country where the theft occurred (Spain), implying the user had to fly back to Spain just to get a police report.
  - **Solution/Workaround**: The Cards Service eventually agreed to process it as a simple "loss" rather than a "theft", bypassing the police report requirement.
  - _Rule of thumb_: If you lose your card abroad and cannot get a local police report immediately, **always report it as "lost" rather than "stolen"** to avoid travel/bureaucratic traps.
  - **Links**:
    - [CERN Admin e-guide on Loss/Theft of Legitimation Documents](https://admin-eguide.web.cern.ch/en/procedure/loss-theft-legitimation-documents-host-states)

### Airline Rejections

- **Context**: Airlines (especially non-EU/Swiss ones, e.g., Saudi Air) frequently refuse boarding to users holding Swiss legitimation cards because they do not recognize them as valid residence permits/visas.
- **Workaround**: Carry a printout or soft copy of the official host-state rules explaining Schengen card types.
  - **Links**:
    - [Users Office Help Crossing Borders](https://usersoffice.web.cern.ch/help-crossing-borders)

## 5. Health Insurance (Uniqa) Coverage Secrets

- **Dermatologist Visits**: Covered by Uniqa, but **only if medically necessary** (not aesthetic). Newcomers should request a written note from the dermatologist stating the treatment was medically required and attach this note to the Uniqa claim.
- **Prescription Glasses**: Covered, but the eye prescription **must be written by an ophthalmologist**, not a standard high-street optician.
- **Extreme Sports (Skiing, Mountain Biking)**: Uniqa covers medical expenses from accidents, but **does not cover search & rescue or repatriation**.
  - _Recommendation_: Join the **Austrian Alpine Club** (ÖAV) or French equivalent. Membership is cheap and includes comprehensive mountain rescue and repatriation insurance.
- **Swiss Insurance Cancellation**: Once Uniqa coverage starts, you can cancel your Swiss health insurance. Uniqa will backwards-cancel and refund if the certificate is delayed.

## 6. Campus Amenities & Life Hacks

### CERN Shuttle & Point 5 Commuting

- **Context**: Interns working at CMS Point 5 face poor public transport options.
- **Details**:
  - The official CERN shuttle to Point 5 (**Circuit 3**) only runs **3 times a day**.
  - Renting a car or carpooling is highly recommended. Supervisors can sometimes arrange a CERN vehicle for work-related commutes between Meyrin and P5.
  - CERN vehicles (with CERN logos) are strictly for work. Using them for personal errands (like shopping at Intermarché) can get you reported and disciplined.
  - **Links**:
    - [CERN Shuttle Circuit 3 Schedule](https://sce-dep.web.cern.ch/mobility/shuttle/circuit-3)

### Showers & Sanitary Facilities

- **Context**: Campus commuters need showers after cycling or running.
- **Details**:
  - Showers are scattered across various buildings.
  - _How to find them_: Go to [maps.cern.ch](https://maps.cern.ch), open the settings/filters, and select **'Sanitary facilities'** to display all toilets and showers on campus.

### Free Bicycle Safety Gear

- **Context**: Bike helmets are technically mandatory on CERN rental bikes.
- **Hack**: The **CERN Mobility Centre** will issue you a rental helmet and reflective safety vest for free upon request, no questions asked.

### Quiet Call Rooms

- **Context**: Finding private places for job interviews or private phone calls.
- **Details**:
  - There are soundproofed phone boxes inside the **CERN Library** (Building 52), operated on a first-come, first-served basis (no booking).
  - Many newer office renovations also have internal phone booths.

### Furniture Disposal (Pays de Gex, France)

- **Context**: Disposing of broken or unwanted furniture when leaving.
- **Details**:
  - Broken items must go to a _déchetterie_ (requires a car). Emmaüs will pick up reusable items for free.
  - _Curbside pickup hack_: Pays de Gex has a free collection service. Fill out the form online, print the code, place up to 3 items on the street, and they will collect it.
  - **Links**:
    - [Pays de Gex Bulky Waste Collection Form](https://demarchesdechets.paysdegex.fr/enlevements)
